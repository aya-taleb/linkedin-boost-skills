# Job spec: linkedin-comment

Written 2026-10-23, before opening any upstream version.

## The job

A founder wants to be seen by the right people on LinkedIn without posting more.
Comments on other people's posts do that, if they add something the post did not have.
The founder pastes a post (or a few), and gets a comment worth reading under each one.

## Who uses it and when

- Daily, during the 15 to 20 minutes of engagement the weekly plan sets aside.
- Before or after their own post goes out.
- When a buyer, partner or peer posts and they want to show up well.

## Inputs

- The post text, pasted. Author name and role if known.
- The active business from founder-context (tone, topics, proof, off-limits).
- Optional: why this person matters (buyer, peer, bigger account), from the plan's engage list.

## Output

- 2 or 3 comment options per post, each doing a different thing, with a pick and why.
- Each one stands alone, names the post's actual point, and stays well under the 1,250
  character comment limit (most good ones are 2 to 5 lines).
- Humanized before showing.
- A line in `comments.md` in the business folder when the user says they posted it.
- If the author could buy: a note on what not to do (no pitch in comments) and when a DM
  could make sense, handing to linkedin-dm.

## Rules

- Never post, never automate, never bulk-comment with a tool.
- Never claim experience, clients or numbers the founder does not have. Use proof from
  founder-context or ask. Missing numbers become `{{your number}}`.
- No pitch, no link to your own product, no "great post".
- Stay out of off-limits topics for the active business.
- If the post is something the founder should not touch (tragedy, politics, a fight), say so.

## Done well looks like

The author would want to reply. A stranger reading only the comment learns something.
The founder could defend every claim in it.
