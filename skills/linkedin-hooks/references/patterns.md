# Hook patterns

Eight ways to open a post, written for this repo. Each one needs a real fact from the
founder. If the fact is missing, the pattern does not fit, or it gets a `{{placeholder}}`.

| pattern | shape | use it when | it goes wrong when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| number | "<number> <thing> in <time>." | you have a real count, amount or time | the number is round, vague or not yours |
| before-after | "<old way>. Now <new way>." | something changed and you can show both sides | the change is tiny or the "before" is made up |
| mistake | "I <did wrong thing>. It cost <cost>." | you can name what it cost | it is a humblebrag dressed as a mistake |
| scene | "<time>, <place>. <one thing happening>." | there is a moment with people in it | it takes three lines to get anywhere |
| said-no | "We said no to <thing most say yes to>." | you turned down money, a client, a feature | there is no reason given right after |
| claim | "<plain statement some readers will push back on>." | you hold a view and have the experience behind it | nobody actually disagrees with it |
| question | "<question only your audience would ask>?" | the question names a real, specific situation | it is "Thoughts?" in a longer coat |
| overheard | "\"<exact words a customer said>\"" | you have their real words and the right to share them | the quote is invented or polished |

Notes:
- Line 1 stands alone. Line 2 pays it off.
- Keep it under about 140 characters so it fits before "see more" on a phone.
- A hook that would work with any other founder's name on it is not finished.
- Never invent a quote for "overheard". Ask for the real words or pick another pattern.
