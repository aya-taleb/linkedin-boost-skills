Talk: "Selling lunch to offices without a sales team" by Rosa Mendes, Seattle Founders Night, 2026-10-09

[00:00] Thanks for having me. I run Lunch Lane. We bring local food trucks to office lobbies once a week.
[01:10] When I started, I emailed 60 offices. Two replied. Both said no.
[02:30] What worked was the front desk. Receptionists know who orders lunch. I stopped emailing and started walking in at 10am with a menu.
[04:05] Our first yes came from an office manager who said, quote, "I just want one lunch I don't have to plan." That sentence is now on our homepage.
[05:40] Trucks were the harder side. Truck owners do not want another app. They want to know the order count by 9am. So we text them by 9am, every time.
[06:50] We have been late twice in 11 weeks. Both were parades. I now check the city events calendar every Sunday.
[08:15] Pricing: I won't share numbers here, but the lesson was to charge per office per week, not per meal, because office managers budget weekly.
[09:30] One more thing, unrelated. My other project, Frameleaf, is an app for kids' story videos. Different audience, I'll talk about it another time.
