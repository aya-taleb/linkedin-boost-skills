# Job spec: linkedin-repurpose

Written 2026-10-23, before opening any upstream version.

## The job

The founder already made something long: a talk, a podcast, a video, a newsletter, a
blog post, a class presentation. They want several LinkedIn posts out of it, each one
standing on its own, without watering it down or making anything up.

## Inputs

- The source text. For video or audio, a transcript (from the transcribe skill, or one
  they exported). Never downloaded from a platform.
- The active business from founder-context, and its `plan.md` and `log.md` if present.

## Output

- A map of the distinct ideas in the source, with where each came from (timestamp or
  paragraph), and which are strong enough to post.
- 3 to 5 standalone post drafts (more only if the source holds them), each one idea, each
  with its own hook, all humanized, facts only from the source.
- Which idea suits a carousel and which clip suits talking-head-cards, if any.
- Drafts saved to `drafts/` in the business folder and offered as plan slots.

## Rules

- Only the founder's own content, or content they have rights to. Someone else's talk
  can be commented on with credit, never passed off.
- Quotes are exact. Paraphrase is marked as paraphrase.
- One business per post. If the source spans two businesses, split the posts.

## Done well looks like

Each post would make sense to someone who never saw the original, and every claim traces
back to a line in the source.
